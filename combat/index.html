Recovered from source

  • Base hero stats = (level benefit + star-step benefit) × template factor / 10,000.
  • Hero bonuses are grouped by source. The sandbox separates growth percentages from flat gear/fighter/decor stats; battle stacking remains an approximation.
  • Soldier stats = (base + flat bonuses) × (1 + percentage bonuses). Power weights: HP 0.5, ATK 12.5, DEF 35; morale contributes zero power.
  • Skill parameters = (star base + star growth × actual skill level) × star factor.
  • Faction counters reduce incoming damage by 20% for the favored matchup. Five matching heroes give +20% HP/ATK/DEF.

Still to establish

The inspected arena client receives timed combat results from the server and plays them back. We have not recovered the server’s full damage formula, DEF/shield rules, target selection or casualty calculation.

Displayed power does not uniquely determine combat stats. The two reports constrain the model; they do not calibrate win probabilities.
